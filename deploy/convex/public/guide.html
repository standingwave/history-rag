The dashboard

One screen, three tiles, reordered by time of day: Agenda always on top, then Tasks and the Digest (evenings flip the last two). Tap any tile to open its full view; search · ask · browse in the corner open the history tools.

Everything is live: change the note on your Mac, tick a box on your phone — every open copy updates within seconds.

Add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and it behaves like an app.

Tasks

The list is your Obsidian daily note — Oriel reads it and writes it back. Tap a circle to tick a task. The floating + adds one; the faint … at a row's end opens edit · + subtask · 📎 attach · delete.

Tap a task's text to expand it: subtasks (tickable, with their own …), attachments, links, and how long it's been on the list.

📎 attach takes a link, a note line, or a photo/file — photos upload with a progress bar and land in the vault's attachments folder.

Every change is applied to the note by your Mac. If the Mac is asleep, changes queue and land when it wakes — see what yellow means.

Timers

Open timers from the dashboard corner. Chips start a countdown in one tap; the custom row takes minutes, an optional label, and a repeat toggle for every-N-minutes reminders (stretch, eye break). ◷ stopwatch counts up instead.

While anything runs, a glowing bar sits at the top of the dashboard with the live countdowns — tap it to get back to the sheet. When a timer finishes it chimes, turns yellow, and pulses until you dismiss it; a repeat chimes each round and rolls straight into the next.

Timers live in Convex, not on one phone: the same countdown ticks on every signed-in device, and pausing or dismissing anywhere updates everywhere.

enable lock-screen alerts (once per device) makes finished timers notify you even when Oriel is closed. On iPhone this needs the app added to the home screen.

A question instead of a query. A model searches your history itself, reads what it finds, and answers with citations you can tap. Pick the model by speed and cost in the dropdown.

What yellow means

Your notes live on your Mac; the phone sends changes to it. The colours tell you where a change is:

○pulsing yellow — sent, in flight; usually settles in seconds ○still yellow · "queued" — the Mac is asleep; it will apply the moment it wakes. Nothing is lost. ▭red box — that change couldn't be applied; the app undid it and says why

The dashboard's Tasks tile shows "· 1 waiting" whenever something is still queued.
